About Me

A historian who digs into primary sources — and helps you prove your line.

George Washington by John Trumbull (1780) — public domain via Wikimedia Commons / The Met

I’m Jonathan Spangler. I live in the Schaumburg, Illinois area and work with clients anywhere. Deep Roots is my genealogy and historical research practice: lineage society applications, family and house histories, archive retrieval, and talks grounded in original records.

I came to client genealogy the long way — through formal history training, years of program and research work, and the stubborn habit of chasing my own Mayflower and SAR lines until the citations held. I care about proving what can be proved, saying clearly what cannot, and leaving you with a report you can hand to a registrar, a relative, or your future self.

Education & credentials

  • Master of Arts in Teaching — History University of Illinois Chicago, May 2020
  • Master of Public Health Emory University, 2006
  • Bachelor of Arts — History & Anthropology University of Wisconsin–Madison, 2000
  • Illinois Professional Educator License (PEL) Senior High; endorsements in Social Science — History, Sociology & Anthropology, and Health

Experience that shapes the work

Before focusing on client genealogy, I spent about sixteen years in global health program management — work that taught me how to plan research carefully, manage timelines and budgets, and write for audiences who need clarity more than flourish.

I have done historical research in primary sources for museums and public-history projects, including science-museum submarine historical research in 2020. On the side I curate and interpret a small private collection of historic sleds; that collector’s habit of reading objects and documents together is the same instinct I bring to deeds, census schedules, and military files — it is not the genealogy business itself, just a reminder that I like evidence you can touch.

As a genealogist I work my own Mayflower and SAR lines, which keeps me honest about what lineage societies actually require. I am fluent in French, with elementary Spanish for reading support when records call for it.

How I like to work with clients

We start with a free 30-minute consultation. If we are a fit, you receive a written scope letter, then a deposit of at least $500 before research begins. You get progress that is documented, not mysterious — and a final product with citations you can follow.
