Pricing

Published rates, a clear deposit rule, and no surprises about third-party fees.

Emancipation Proclamation manuscript page — public domain via Wikimedia Commons / WDL

Base rate

$60 per hour for research, analysis, writing, and most retrieval visits.

Flat packages are listed below for well-scoped work. Complex or open-ended projects stay hourly.

$500 minimum deposit. No research begins until a deposit of at least $500 is received. For hourly work, that deposit is a retainer applied to billed hours (additional blocks are paid in advance). For flat packages of $500 or more, pay 50% to start and the balance before final delivery. Smaller flat items under $500 (such as the $175 eligibility check or a single talk) are paid in full before work or the event.

What the deposit covers

  • Hourly retainers are applied to research and reporting time at $60/hr.
  • Flat-fee deposits reserve the project and cover early research; the balance is due before you receive the final packet or narrative.
  • Unused retainer time can roll into a follow-on block by agreement; unused balances on canceled work are handled in the scope letter.

Billed separately (at cost)

  • Vital-record, certificate, and archive copy fees
  • Lineage society application and national dues
  • Subscriptions purchased solely for one client
  • Postage, parking, and mileage beyond 30 miles (IRS rate)
  • Out-of-area travel only with a quote you approve first

What you receive

Every research engagement includes a written scope letter up front and a cited report (or society packet) at the end. You pay for professional time and documentation — not a promised outcome. That matches Association of Professional Genealogists norms.
